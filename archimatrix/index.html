About

The Studio

We create interiors and architecture that balance form, function, and emotion. Guided by light, material, and proportion, our work is dedicated to shaping timeless spaces that feel both purposeful and inspiring.

Since 2000, Archimatrix has shaped homes, workplaces and neighbourhoods across Malaysia - architecture unbound by any single style, type or scale, and focused on making the most of time, space and resources.
